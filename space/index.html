Fracture X-ray Check

ConvNeXt-Tiny classifier and YOLO11s detector trained on FracAtlas (hand, leg, hip, shoulder). Runs entirely in your browser; the image is not uploaded anywhere.

Research demo, not a medical device. Scores are model outputs against one dataset from three hospitals in Bangladesh. They are not a diagnosis and must not be used for patient care.
Drop an X-ray image here (JPEG or PNG), or click to choose a file
Samples are FracAtlas test images: the clearest fracture, a fracture the model is unsure about, and the clearest normal image.

How to read this. The probability is temperature-scaled on validation data. The verdict uses the validation threshold. If the model's confidence (the larger of p and 1 − p) is below 0.8 it asks for a clinician instead of answering. Boxes come from a separate detector and are less reliable than the classifier.

Code and paper: GitHub · model: Hugging Face · data: FracAtlas (Abedeen et al., 2023, CC BY 4.0).
